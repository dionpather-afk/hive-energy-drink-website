Our story

Energy is better together.

A hive only works when everyone moves as one. That idea is behind every can.

Why Hive?

Bees are small, but together they never stop. They build, they work and they look after their own. We wanted a drink that carries that same spirit: for the people who keep moving, and the people who move with them.

That is why the can is black and gold, and why the honeycomb sits on every one. It is a badge for the crew, the team and the community you belong to.
